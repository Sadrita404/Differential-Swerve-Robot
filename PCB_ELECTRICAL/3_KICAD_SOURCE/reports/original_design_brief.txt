Project Brief: 12-Channel Stepper Driver Carrier PCB

Objective

Design a PCB that hosts up to 12 socketed stepstick driver modules (A4988 or TMC2209, mixed freely per channel) with per-channel microstepping configuration, a single consolidated control interface for STEP/DIR, and a high-current shared motor power bus. The board is a passive carrier: no MCU onboard. Deliverable is a complete KiCad project ready for fab.

Functional Requirements

Driver sockets (x12)


Two 1x8 female headers per channel, standard stepstick footprint. Must accept both A4988 and TMC2209 modules without modification.
Orientation marking on silkscreen (EN pin corner). Wrong insertion destroys drivers, so make this unmissable.
RST and SLP pins tied together via a solder jumper (default: bridged). This is required for A4988 operation and is safe for TMC2209. Do not hard-trace it: leave the jumper cuttable and route the SLP-position pin (PDN_UART on TMC2209) to an optional labeled pad per channel for future UART use.


Microstepping (per channel)


3-position DIP switch connecting MS1/MS2/MS3 to VDD. Both driver families have internal pulldowns; no external resistors.
Silkscreen a microstep truth table for BOTH driver types adjacent to each switch or once in a legend area. The tables differ; label them clearly (A4988 vs TMC2209, note MS3 = SPREAD on TMC2209 and should stay OFF for stealthChop).


Enable


All 12 EN pins bused together (active low, both families).
3-pin header: center = EN bus, one side = GND (jumper here = always enabled), other side = breakout to control header for MCU-driven global enable.


Control interface


2x13 (26-pin) shrouded IDC header carrying 12x STEP, 12x DIR, 1x GND, 1x 5V VDD sense/supply pin. If pin budget is tight, prioritize interleaved GND returns over the VDD pin and move VDD to a separate 2-pin header.
Consistent channel ordering (CH1–CH12) matching physical socket order; silkscreen the pinout next to the header.
No series resistors required, but leave 0-ohm/resistor footprints (0603) on STEP lines in case termination is needed later. DNP by default.


Logic power (VDD)


5V input via 2-pin header or screw terminal, feeding all socket VDD pins.
Budget: ~200 mA total. 100 nF ceramic at each socket VDD pin.
Reverse-polarity protection optional; at minimum silkscreen polarity clearly.


Motor power (VMOT)


Single shared bus, up to 30 V, sized for 12 A continuous (design target ~1 A per channel).
Driver voltage limits: A4988 max 35 V, TMC2209 max 28 V operating (29 V absolute max). Above 28 V the board is A4988-only. Silkscreen this limit near the power terminal.
Input: 5.08 mm pitch high-current screw terminal, 16 A+ rated. Consider dual entry (terminals at both ends of the bus) so no single trace segment carries full current.
Bulk capacitance: 1000 µF 50 V low-ESR at the input. Suggested: Panasonic EEU-FR1H102 (12.5 mm dia) or Nichicon UPW1H102MHD.
Per channel: 100 µF 50 V low-ESR electrolytic placed directly at the socket VMOT/GND pins (hard requirement, this protects A4988s from inductive spikes) plus 100 nF ceramic. Suggested: Panasonic EEU-FR1H101 (8 × 11.5 mm) or Rubycon 50ZLH100MEFC8X11.5.
Optional but preferred: blade fuse or polyfuse on VMOT input, TVS diode SMBJ30A across the bus.


Motor connectors (x12)


JST-XH 4-pin (B4B-XH-A) footprint per channel. The 2.5 mm XH pitch is compatible enough with 2.54 mm headers that either can be populated on the same footprint; confirm this in footprint review.
Phase pairs labeled on silkscreen (A1 A2 B1 B2, matching the socket's 1A/1B/2A/2B pinout).


Layout Requirements


Board size target: ~110 × 120 mm, 2×6 channel grid, motor connectors exiting top and bottom edges, IDC header on one side edge, power terminal center-fed between the two rows. Do not shrink below ~100 × 110 mm; the 12 A VMOT pour and per-channel electrolytics need the area. Channel cell budget: ~18 mm pitch × ~50 mm.
2 oz copper, 2-layer minimum. VMOT as a wide pour on top, solid GND pour on bottom, stitched with vias. Do not neck VMOT below ~4 mm equivalent width anywhere on the main bus.
Motor phase traces: 1.5–2 mm.
Channel layout: draw one channel as a hierarchical schematic sheet, instance 12x. Route one channel's local layout, then replicate (KiCad "Replicate Layout" plugin) to keep all 12 identical.
Physical grouping per channel: socket + DIP switch + electrolytic cap + JST connector in one tight cluster. Electrolytic as close to socket VMOT pins as physically possible.
Board edges: control IDC header on one edge, motor connectors along the opposite/adjacent edges fanning outward, power terminal(s) central to the VMOT bus, not in a far corner.
Mounting holes in all four corners, M3.
Silkscreen: channel numbers, all pinouts, microstep tables, driver orientation, VMOT voltage range, and a warning: "Do not connect/disconnect motors while powered."
